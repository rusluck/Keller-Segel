A Linear ODE is defined as
$$y'=f(x)y.$$
By seperation of variables,
$$\frac{y'}{y}=f(x)$$
and by integrating both sides,
$$\ln(y)=F(x)+C$$
where $F(x)$ is some antiderivative of $f(x)$.
Then exponentiating both sides and choosing new constants,
$$y=Ce^{F(x)}.$$
Therefore $y$ must always be positive.
